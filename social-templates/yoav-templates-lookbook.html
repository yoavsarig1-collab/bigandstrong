Lookbook · four directions

Words worth slowing down for.

Four post templates for Instagram & Facebook (4:5, 1080×1350). They share one design language so a reader knows it’s yours before reading a word — then each differs in style, so your feed stays alive.

What every post shares

  • A pause cue — three fading dots that say “breathe, this one’s worth it.”
  • Key-letter emphasis — weighted opening letters guide a fast first read, then invite a slow one.
  • Air & spacing — roomy letters and lines; one thought per post.
  • Text that curves — arcs, waves, steps, a tilt. Never a straight block.
  • Nature ground and the same hand-signed yoav leaf mark.

The writing shown is sample copy to demonstrate the form. On a desktop browser you can open the editable canvas to change any text in place.

Horizon

warm · literary

Warm sand, a rising-sun glow, topographic rings. Cormorant Garamond italic on an arc.

Best for  a gentle turn, reassurance, hope.

on  time

We think we are chasing the day. But the day is a river — and we are the ones still standing on the bank, dry, afraid to step in.

Grove

sage · grounded

Sage green, swaying botanicals. Marcellus in stepped, offset lines with a Work Sans body.

Best for  a clear idea you want to land.

on  attention
What you
water,
grows.

Your attention is not free. It is the only soil you own. Plant it carefully — and only where you want a forest.

yoav

Tide

dusk · weighty

A dusk palette with drifting tide lines. Spectral italic riding a wave — “this is important.”

Best for  depth, loss, courage.

on  fear

Nothing you love leaves for good. It goes out, so that it can come back and find you still standing on the shore.

yoav

Field

airy · minimal

The quietest. Playfair Display, one big tilted thought, drifting motes, a single sprig.

Best for  a single line that should breathe.

on  stillness
Be a field,
not a race.

A field does not hurry the spring. It simply stays open, and lets the season do its slow, certain work.
